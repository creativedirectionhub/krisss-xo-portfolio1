    Questions

    Good to know

    Do you need the product?

    Yes for physical products. Ship it and I keep it; the product isn't counted as payment. For apps and services, a login or account is enough.

    Will the video be posted on your TikTok?

    Not by default. UGC is made for your brand to post. Posting on @krisss.xo is a $75 add-on.

    What does "usage rights" mean?

    Posting on your own pages is included. Running the video as a paid ad, or from my account through Spark Ads, is priced by how long you run it.

    Can you write the script?

    Yes. Every video includes the concept and hook. If you have a script or talking points, I'll work from those instead.

    How do revisions work?

    One round is included per video for edits like trims, captions and order of shots. Reshoots or extra rounds are $50.

    How do I pay?

    A 50% deposit reserves your spot, and the balance is due on delivery. Invoices are sent by email.
